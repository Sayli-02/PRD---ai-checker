# Copy rules

All landing copy lives in `content/site.ts`. Components only read it.

## Voice

- Short sentences. No jargon above the fold.
- Indian English: "optimise", "colour", "centre".
- The visitor is a busy business owner on a phone. Say what they get, plainly.
- Make clear our team does the work; the client never does SEO themselves.

## Never

- **Prices or fees** anywhere on the page.
- **Guaranteed results** or rank promises: no "guaranteed", "#1", "top spot", "100%", "instantly".
- **Fake proof**: no invented testimonials, client logos, case studies or "checks run" numbers. Show them only when real.
- **AI-hype words**: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "cutting-edge", "supercharge", "unlock", "leverage".
- **Jargon unexplained**: write "AEO (getting named in AI answers)" the first time; "SEO" is fine in body copy, never in the brand name.

## Check results wording

Results are "a snapshot today". AI answers change between runs, so never promise a ranking or say a score "is" the business's visibility.

## Consent and data

Say why each field is asked. The consent checkbox covers calls and WhatsApp messages; store the consent time (India DPDP Act).

## Quick test before committing copy

1. Could a 10-year-old follow the sentence?
2. Does it promise an outcome? Rewrite as what we do.
3. Is any number or name real and sourced? If not, remove it.
