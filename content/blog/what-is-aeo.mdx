AEO, or Answer Engine Optimisation, is the work of making your business easy for AI assistants to find, trust and name when someone asks them a question. If a customer asks ChatGPT for "the best dental clinic in Pune" and your clinic is in the answer, AEO is part of the reason. If it is not, AEO is the gap to close.

## What does AEO mean in simple words?

SEO helps your pages show up in a list of links on Google. AEO helps your business show up inside a short answer written by an AI assistant. The customer does not scroll a list. They read one answer, and they usually act on the first names in it.

An "answer engine" is any tool that answers a question directly instead of listing links. ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity are the best known. Google also shows AI-written summaries in some search results, which its own documentation calls [AI features in Search](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

## Why should a business owner care about AEO?

Because the first step of buying is starting to move. People who used to type "dentist near me" into Google now also ask an assistant in full sentences: "Which dentist in Pune is good with children and has evening appointments?" The assistant gives a short list. If you are on it, you may get the call. If you are not, you never find out that customer existed.

Nobody can give you an exact count of how many of your customers do this. It differs by city and by type of business. ## How do AI assistants decide which businesses to name?

Each tool works differently, and none publishes its full method. Be careful with anyone who claims to know the exact formula. A few things hold true across most of them:

- They lean on what is written about you across the web, not only on your own website.
- Clear, consistent facts are easier to repeat correctly than vague ones. Your name, address, services and the areas you serve should read the same everywhere.
- Tools that search the web while they answer, such as Perplexity, show their sources. Pages that answer the question directly are easier for them to quote.
- Reviews, listings and mentions on trusted sites back up what you say about yourself.

## What does AEO work actually involve?

Good AEO is mostly careful, ordinary work done well. It starts with a look at how AI tools describe you today, then fixes the gaps one by one.

| Area | What we look at | A typical fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your website | Does each service have its own clear page? | Add a page for "root canal treatment in Pune" |
| Business listings | Are your name, address and phone the same everywhere? | Correct old or duplicate listings |
| Trusted sources | Which sites do AI tools quote in your category? | Complete your profile on those sites |
| Content | Do you answer the questions customers really ask? | Add short, direct answers to common questions |
| Structure | Can machines read what your pages are about? | Add markup, as Google explains in its [guide to structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/intro-structured-data) |

None of this is a trick. It makes your business clearer to people and to machines at the same time.

## What does AEO look like for a real business?

Here are three examples. They are illustrations, not client results.

- **A dental clinic in Pune.** The clinic has one "Services" page and an old phone number on two directories. Assistants describe it vaguely or skip it. AEO work gives each treatment its own page, fixes the phone number everywhere, and adds short answers to questions such as "Is a root canal painful?"
- **A coaching centre in Jaipur.** Parents ask for "the best coaching for class 10 maths". The centre's results and batch details are in a PDF no one can read. AEO work turns that into clear web pages with the course, batch timings and location in plain text.
- **A restaurant in Hyderabad.** Diners ask for "family restaurants with parking near Banjara Hills". The menu is only an image. AEO work puts the cuisine, area, parking and opening hours into readable text and keeps them identical on every listing.

In each case the change is simple. The business becomes easier to understand, for people and for machines.

## What are common myths about AEO?

- **"It is a trick to game AI."** It is not. It is clear, accurate information shared in the places AI tools look.
- **"It is only for big brands."** Local businesses can benefit, because many customer questions name a city and a service.
- **"Once done, it is done."** Answers and sources change, so the work needs tracking every month.
- **"It replaces SEO."** It does not. Customers still search on Google.

## Is AEO different from SEO?

They overlap a great deal. A clear, helpful, well-organised website helps in both. The difference is the goal. SEO aims to rank a page for a search. AEO aims to get your business named and cited in an answer. Most businesses need both, because customers still use Google and are also starting to ask AI. Read [SEO vs AEO](/blog/seo-vs-aeo) for a side-by-side comparison.

## Can anyone guarantee a mention in ChatGPT?

No. AI answers can change from one run to the next, even for the same question, and the tools are run by other companies that change them without notice. A trustworthy agency will not promise a mention or a rank. What it can do is improve the odds, measure the result and track it over time.

## What is a good first step?

Start by seeing where you stand.

1. Pick three questions a customer in your city might ask, such as "best [your service] in [your city]".
2. Ask each one in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.
3. Write down who is named and whether you are on the list.
4. Note the websites the answers mention or link to.
