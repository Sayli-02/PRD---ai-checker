SEO gets your pages ranked in Google's list of links. AEO gets your business named inside the answer an AI assistant gives. They share a lot of groundwork, so most businesses do not have to choose. They need both, in the right order.

## What is SEO?

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimisation. It is the work of making your website easy for Google to understand and worth showing when someone searches. A customer types "interior designer in Nagpur", Google shows a list of links, and the customer picks one. Good SEO helps your link appear higher in that list.

Google's own [SEO starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) covers the basics: clear page titles, helpful content, a site that works well on phones and links from other sites.

## What is AEO?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimisation. A customer asks an assistant, "Which interior designer in Nagpur is good for small flats?" and gets one written answer that names a few businesses. AEO is the work of making sure your business is one of the names, and that the facts about it are right. We explain this fully in [What is AEO?](/blog/what-is-aeo)

## How are SEO and AEO different?

| | SEO | AEO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where it works | Google search results | ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity answers, and AI summaries in search |
| What the customer sees | A list of links to choose from | One short answer naming a few businesses |
| The goal | Rank a page for a search | Be named and cited in the answer |
| What we work on | Pages, keywords, speed, links | Clear facts, trusted sources, pages that are easy to quote |
| How we measure | Rankings, visits, calls from search | How often you are named, who is named instead, which sources are quoted |

The biggest practical difference is the measure. In SEO you can look up your position. In AEO there is no fixed position, because answers change between runs. So we measure how often you are named across many questions, as a snapshot, and track it over time.

## Where do SEO and AEO overlap?

More than most people expect. Both reward a business that is clear, accurate and helpful.

- **A clear website.** A page for each service, with your city and address in plain text, helps Google and helps AI tools describe you correctly.
- **Helpful content.** Google's guidance on [creating helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) applies to both. Pages written for real customers do better than pages written for machines.
- **Trust.** Honest reviews, complete listings and mentions on respected sites support your name in both places.

If you do the shared basics well, you have already done a large part of both jobs.

## What does AEO add on top of SEO?

AEO adds extra attention to a few things that matter more when a machine has to summarise you in one or two sentences.

1. **Consistent facts.** Your name, address, phone number, services and the areas you serve should match across your website and your listings. Conflicting details make an assistant less sure.
2. **Direct answers.** A short, clear answer to a question, placed near the top of a page, is easier to quote than a long story.
3. **The sources AI trusts.** In many categories an assistant leans on a few directories, review sites or news pages. Knowing which ones, and being present and accurate there, helps.
4. **Readable structure.** Markup that tells machines what a page is about, such as FAQ and organisation details, removes guesswork.

## What do SEO and AEO look like for one business?

Take a coaching centre in Jaipur as an illustration. It is an example, not a client story.

On the SEO side, the centre wants to appear when a parent searches "class 10 maths coaching in Jaipur". Work here covers a clear page for that course, a fast mobile site, a complete Google Business Profile and links from local education sites.

On the AEO side, the same parent asks an assistant, "Which coaching centre in Jaipur is good for class 10 maths and has small batches?" Work here makes sure the batch size, subjects, location and results are stated clearly in text, are identical on every listing, and appear on the sites the assistant tends to quote.

The two lists share many items. The AEO list simply adds more attention to exact facts and to the outside sources behind them.

## What mistakes do businesses make?

1. **Treating them as rivals.** Spending on one and ignoring the other leaves a gap. They support each other.
2. **Chasing a rank for AI.** There is no fixed rank in an AI answer. Measure how often you are named across many questions instead.
3. **Writing for machines.** Pages stuffed with keywords help neither Google nor assistants. Write for a customer, then make the facts clear.
4. **Never rechecking.** Both search and AI answers change. A yearly look is not enough.

## Which one should you do first?

Start with the shared basics, because they pay off twice.

1. Make sure your Google Business Profile is complete and correct.
2. Give each main service its own clear page with your city named.
3. Add short, honest answers to the five questions customers ask most.
4. Check how AI tools describe you today with the [free AI Visibility Check](/#check).

Then add the AEO-specific work once the basics are solid: tidy listings, the sources AI trusts and structured data.
