You can see whether AI recommends your business in about ten minutes with no special tools. Ask a handful of customer-style questions in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, write down who is named, and count how often it is you. This guide shows the method step by step.

## What do you need before you start?

Just three things: a free account or access to each assistant, a notebook or spreadsheet, and ten minutes. Open a fresh chat for every question so earlier answers do not influence the next. If an assistant offers personalised memory or history, turn it off or use a private window, because personalised answers will not match what a new customer sees.

## Which questions should you ask?

Use the words a real customer would use, not the words you would use. Think about how people in your city actually ask. Here are five patterns, with a dental clinic in Pune as the example.

| # | Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Best in the city | "Best dental clinics in Pune" |
| 2 | Top rated, nearby | "Top rated dental clinics near me in Pune" |
| 3 | Choosing between options | "Which dental clinic in Pune should I choose" |
| 4 | Trust and reviews | "Most trusted dental clinics in Pune with good reviews" |
| 5 | A direct recommendation | "Recommend a good dental clinic in Pune" |

Replace "dental clinic" and "Pune" with your own category and city. If you serve a specific area or need, add one more question, such as "dental clinic in Kothrud for children".

## How do you record the answers?

Make a simple table with one row per answer. Fifteen rows give you a fair picture: five questions across three assistants.

| Question | Assistant | Were you named? | Your position | Others named | Sources mentioned |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Best dental clinics in Pune | ChatGPT | Yes or no | 1, 2, 3 or none | Names | Websites shown |

Be exact about position. If the assistant gives a numbered list, note where you appear. If it gives a paragraph, note the order in which names come up.

## How do you work out your score?

Keep it simple. Count how many of your fifteen answers name your business.

- 0 to 3 answers: you are rarely named.
- 4 to 8 answers: you are named sometimes.
- 9 to 15 answers: you are named often.

These bands are a rough guide, not an industry standard. Being named first or second matters more than being named fifth, so make a note of that too. The [free AI Visibility Check](/#check) uses a similar idea and gives extra weight to a top-three place.

## What should you look for besides your own name?

The most useful finding is often not whether you appear, but who does and why.

1. **Competitors named instead.** Which businesses show up again and again? Visit their websites and listings and compare them with yours.
2. **The reasons given.** Assistants usually add a short reason, such as "known for paediatric care". These reasons show which facts get repeated.
3. **The sources.** Perplexity lists its sources, and others sometimes do. If the same directory or review site appears often, make sure your profile there is complete.
4. **Wrong facts.** Look for an old address, a missing service or a closed branch. Wrong facts are quick wins to fix.

## What does a worked example look like?

This is an invented example to show the method. Suppose a dental clinic in Pune records fifteen answers.

| Assistant | Answers that named the clinic (out of 5) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | 2 | Named third and fifth |
| Gemini | 1 | Named fourth |
| Perplexity | 1 | Named second, with a link to a directory page |

That is four of fifteen answers, so the clinic is named sometimes. Looking closer, two competitors appear in most answers, and one directory is quoted several times. The clinic's own profile on that directory has an old phone number. The first fix is clear: correct the directory profile, then add a dedicated page for its main treatments.

## What mistakes should you avoid when checking?

- **Asking leading questions.** "Is Dr Rao's clinic the best?" tells you nothing. Ask the open question a stranger would ask.
- **Using one chat for everything.** Earlier answers shape later ones. Start fresh each time.
- **Checking once.** One run is one snapshot. Repeat on another day before drawing conclusions.
- **Ignoring the sources.** Your own position matters, but the sources explain why.
- **Panicking over one miss.** Not being named in one answer is normal. Look at the pattern across all fifteen.

## Why will your answers differ from someone else's?

AI answers vary between runs, between accounts and over time. A different phone, a different location or a different day can change the names. Because of this, treat your result as a snapshot of today. If you want to track progress, repeat the same questions every month and compare the pattern, not one single answer.

## What should you do with the result?

Pick one fix and do it this week.

- If you are rarely named, start with a clear page for each main service, naming your city.
- If wrong facts appear, correct your website and listings first.
- If one site is quoted often, complete your profile there.
- If you are named sometimes, look at which questions you miss and add direct answers to them.
